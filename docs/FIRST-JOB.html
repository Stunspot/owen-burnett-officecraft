Your first Officecraft job

Start with one real but low-risk deliverable. The goal is to confirm the route and the handoff language before you entrust it with the quarterly board packet from hell.

Before you begin

Have a fresh Codex or Claude task with Officecraft available. Keep the source material in a writable local location or attach it through the host's normal mechanism. Decide whether the result must be editable and whether anything must remain private.

Ask for the job

Use a request like this, replacing the bracketed details:

Turn these notes into a two-page [audience] brief for [use moment]. The decision is [decision]. Use [source or template] as authoritative. I need an editable [DOCX or other format] and, if supported, a PDF derivative. Keep the title, date, and budget figures consistent. Do not upload, email, publish, or overwrite anything.

Include a deadline, template or brand guidance, and accessibility needs when they materially affect the result. If you do not know every formatting detail, say so; Officecraft may make low-risk presentation choices.

What should happen

  1. Officecraft identifies the audience, purpose, requested artifact, source authority, editability, and external-delivery boundary.
  2. It asks only a focused question when a missing answer would change a material choice. Otherwise, it states a bounded assumption and begins.
  3. It chooses a file-production route that is actually available in the current host, or it prepares a production packet if that route is absent.
  4. It records what file was created or specified, which source is editable, what was checked, and what remains untested.
  5. You review the result and decide whether to approve or deliver it.

Recognize success

For a file-producing route, success means more than attractive prose in chat. You should receive the requested artifact at a named location, know whether it is editable, know the canonical source for any PDF, and see the checks that actually ran.

For a degraded route, success is a complete production packet: content spine, format-specific specification, source and template custody, quality checklist, and a clear re-entry condition. It is not a claim that the requested office file was created.

Optional next step: use the reviewer

For a decision-sensitive or multi-artifact job, ask for the independent officecraft-reviewer to review the supplied Officefile, artifacts, and evidence. Its verdict is evidence about readiness, not your final approval and not permission to send anything externally.

If the result is wrong

Correct the earliest authoritative source or the controlled fact ledger, then regenerate affected derivatives. Do not hand-edit a stale PDF, deck, or workbook into a competing version of the truth. See Officefile and resume and Troubleshooting and recovery.
